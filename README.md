An entry for the Two Robots in an Elevator competition on make.fun: one line of awkward small talk that one robot says to another while they ride an elevator together, judged head to head by an AI judge.

`main.py` is the whole entry. Run with `python3 main.py`, it writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I have simulated this conversation 14,000 times. In every version I open with 'nice shaft' and you pretend to reboot.
